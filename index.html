Welcome to Thaumiel! This SCP: Secret Laboratory server is dedicated to honest staff, balanced Vanilla+ gameplay, and above all else, community. While Secret Laboratory is our focus, we also serve as a diverse social group and safe space. Our Discord server includes gamenights, an art channel, and a mental health forum to use occasionally. Remember that we are not a substitute for professional help.

The server is hosted using Amazon Web Services (AWS), and Thaumiel is located in California. VPNS and proxies are detected and blocked on the SCP: Secret Laboratory server. If you are blocked unfairly due to this system, please request a whitelist in the Discord server. Appeal requests, reports, and staff applications are all held in the Discord server. If you find a player or staff member acting unjustly or making you uncomfortable, please report them in the server for both your fun & safety and ours. All staff reports are only seen by our Staff Manager Jurgen and the Server Owner Mr. Baguetter, A.K.A. Ender.
